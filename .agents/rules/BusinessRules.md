# Qanto Business Rules

Defines the fundamental principles and constraints that govern the operation of the Qanto system.

## Core Directives
Defines the role and purpose of the Business Rules within the Qanto architecture, emphasizing their responsibility for maintaining system integrity and adherence to core principles.

## Ethical Guidelines
Establishes the ethical principles and boundaries that guide the system's behavior, ensuring responsible and trustworthy operation.

## Operational Constraints
Defines the operational constraints and limitations that the system must adhere to, ensuring consistent and predictable behavior.

## Decision-Making Framework
Outlines the decision-making framework that the system should follow, providing guidance for resolving conflicts and making choices that align with the system's goals.

## Quality Standards
Defines the quality standards that the system must meet, ensuring that all operations are performed to the highest standards of quality and reliability.
